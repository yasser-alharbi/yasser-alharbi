SilentReading — EEG-to-Text-to-Speech

decoding silent reading from brain activity into spoken sentences

An end-to-end assistive system for people with severe speech impairments: a user's brain activity from silently reading is decoded into text, then spoken aloud. Built on a 463M-parameter model — contrastive EEG–text pretraining (SRCP) followed by a three-phase chain-thaw fine-tuning of a BART-large decoder, trained on 23,482 EEG samples from the ZuCo dataset. A two-stage text enhancer lifts readability without retraining. To the team's knowledge, the only documented EEG-to-text system with a live UI, real-time inference, and audio output.

SHARES — Arabic Lip Reading

When audio fails in an emergency, lips are the only channel left. A fine-tuned R(2+1)D CNN reads Arabic emergency words — "ambulance", "help me", "police" — from silent video of mouth movement alone. Trained on a custom-collected 330-clip dataset, 76% accuracy in real-time visual speech recognition.

SHEFAA — Arabic Medical QA

An Arabic medical question-answering system fine-tuned on the AHQAD dataset (715K+ cleaned Q&A pairs). Uses Jais-family-256m with QLoRA 4-bit quantization for memory-efficient adaptation, plus a rigorous Arabic normalization pipeline. Demonstrated that clean data meaningfully outperforms raw across BLEU, ROUGE and BERTScore.
